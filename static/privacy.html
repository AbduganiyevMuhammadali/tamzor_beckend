Ta'm — Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 19-noyabr

"Ta'm" mobil ilovasi (keyingi o'rinlarda — Ilova) Tamzor kompaniyasining xodimlari (savdo agentlari, yetkazib beruvchilar va boshqa ish rollari) uchun mo'ljallangan ichki ish vositasidir. Ilovadan faqat kompaniya bergan login va parol bilan foydalanish mumkin. Ilovada ommaviy ro'yxatdan o'tish yo'q.

1. Qanday ma'lumotlar yig'iladi

  • Hisob ma'lumotlari: login, parol (serverga tekshirish uchun yuboriladi, qurilmada saqlanmaydi), kirish tokeni.
  • Joylashuv (aniq, GPS): faqat xodimning ish vaqtida (ish vaqti kompaniya tomonidan belgilanadi) va faqat kompaniya joylashuvni kuzatishni yoqib qo'ygan xodimlar uchun. Ilova fonda (yopiq turganda) ham joylashuvni yozishi mumkin — bu holatda telefonda doimiy bildirishnoma ko'rinib turadi. Joylashuv, shuningdek, mijozga tashrif, sotuv va yetkazish qayd etilganda yoziladi.
  • Ish ma'lumotlari: sotuvlar, buyurtmalar, yetkazishlar, to'lovlar, qaytarilgan tovarlar, mijozlarga tashriflar.
  • Mijozlar haqidagi ma'lumotlar: xodim kiritgan mijoz nomi, telefon raqami, manzili va joylashuvi (kompaniyaning mijozlari — yuridik shaxslar va do'konlar).
  • Texnik ma'lumotlar: ilova versiyasi va operatsion tizim turi (Android/iOS) — moslikni tekshirish uchun.

2. Ruxsatlar

  • Joylashuv (shu jumladan "Doim"/fonda) — ish vaqtidagi marshrutni yozish uchun.
  • Kamera — server sozlamasi QR kodini skanerlash uchun. Rasmlar saqlanmaydi va yuborilmaydi.
  • Bluetooth va mahalliy tarmoq — chekni printerga chiqarish uchun.
  • Bildirishnomalar — fonda joylashuv yozilayotganini ko'rsatish uchun.

3. Ma'lumotlardan foydalanish

Ma'lumotlar faqat kompaniyaning ish jarayonlari uchun ishlatiladi: savdo va yetkazishni hisobga olish, ombor va kassa hisobi, xodimlar marshrutini nazorat qilish, hisobotlar. Ma'lumotlar reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi va sotilmaydi.

4. Saqlash va uzatish

  • Ma'lumotlar kompaniyaning o'z serveriga yuboriladi va u yerda saqlanadi.
  • Internet bo'lmaganda ma'lumotlar vaqtincha qurilmada saqlanadi va aloqa tiklanganda serverga yuboriladi.
  • Ma'lumotlar uchinchi shaxslarga berilmaydi. Istisno: qonun talab qilgan hollar.
  • Xarita OpenStreetMap xizmatidan yuklanadi. Xarita plitkalarini yuklashda qurilmaning IP manzili OpenStreetMap serverlariga ko'rinadi. Xodim yoki mijoz ma'lumotlari ularga yuborilmaydi.

5. Saqlash muddati va o'chirish

Ish ma'lumotlari kompaniyaning buxgalteriya va hisob talablari muddatida saqlanadi. Xodim hisobini kompaniya administratori yaratadi va o'chiradi. Ma'lumotlarni ko'rish, tuzatish yoki o'chirishni so'rash uchun kompaniya administratoriga yoki quyidagi manzilga murojaat qiling. Ilovadan chiqishda (logout) kirish ma'lumotlari qurilmadan o'chiriladi. Ilovani o'chirib tashlash qurilmadagi barcha ma'lumotlarini o'chiradi.

6. Bolalar

Ilova faqat kompaniya xodimlari uchun mo'ljallangan va 18 yoshdan kichiklar uchun emas.

Ta'm — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

The "Ta'm" mobile app is an internal work tool for employees of Tamzor (sales agents, delivery drivers and other staff roles). It can only be used with a login and password issued by the company. There is no public sign-up.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: login, password (sent to the server for verification, not stored on the device), session token.
  • Precise location (GPS): only during the employee's working hours (set by the company), and only for employees whose location tracking the company has enabled. The app may record location in the background (when closed). While it does, an ongoing notification is shown. Location is also recorded with client visits, sales and deliveries.
  • Work data: sales, orders, deliveries, payments, returns, client visits.
  • Client data: client name, phone number, address and location entered by the employee (the company's business clients and shops).
  • Technical data: app version and operating system type (Android/iOS), for compatibility checks.

2. Permissions

  • Location (including "Always"/background) — to record the work-hours route.
  • Camera — only to scan the server-setup QR code. No photos are stored or sent.
  • Bluetooth and local network — to print receipts.
  • Notifications — to show that background location is active.

3. How data is used

Data is used only for the company's operations: sales and delivery records, stock and cash accounting, employee route monitoring and reports. Data is not used for advertising and is never sold.

4. Storage and sharing

  • Data is sent to and stored on the company's own server.
  • Without internet, data is kept on the device temporarily and sent when the connection returns.
  • Data is not shared with third parties, except where required by law.
  • Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. When the app loads them, OpenStreetMap's servers can see the device's IP address. No employee or client data is sent to them.

5. Retention and deletion

Work data is kept for as long as the company's accounting requirements demand. The company administrator creates and deletes employee accounts. To access, correct or delete your data, contact your company administrator or the contact below. Logging out removes the session from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

6. Children

The app is intended only for company employees and is not for anyone under 18.
